Three lines of work. Each one is a full interior: drawing, material, light, and the way the room is used every day. Add notes with your manager here.

Hospitality

Arrival halls, dining rooms, conservatories built as infrastructure for a stay. The first ten metres have to work: door, light, desk, the garden sitting in the room.

We hold joinery, stone and steel as one operation so the stay does not break between rooms. Typical briefs: boutique houses, dining rooms, lobbies that must feel private.

Wellness

Night gyms and linear light, connected to how the body actually moves. A lane instead of a leftover corridor. Training, recovery and reception on one temperature of room.

Typical briefs: athletic clubs, private gyms, recovery rooms that cannot look like a basement.

Private water

Baths that are rooms, not fixtures. Marble, brass and one window. Plumbing and stone coordinated so nothing reads as an afterthought.

Typical briefs: private baths, hammam-scale wet rooms, dressing thresholds that belong to the water.
